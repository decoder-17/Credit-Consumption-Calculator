How credits are calculated. Each usage type draws credits at a graduated multiplier that steps down as that type's monthly credit consumption crosses tier thresholds — base ≤300K, tier 2 ≤900K, tier 3 ≤5M, tier 4 beyond — like tax brackets, not a flat rate. Thresholds are per usage type and reset on the 1st of each calendar month; multipliers apply across all environments (prod, pre-prod, sandbox, design). Rates shown are from the Mule Credit Rate Card (May 2026).

What to enter. Billable units as they appear in Anypoint Usage Reports — this tool prices a given unit figure; it does not reproduce the raw→billable aggregation (daily-max flows, space-days, message counts). Read those from Usage Reports and enter them here.

Digital Wallet. The Salesforce Digital Wallet is the billing source of truth. It shows your pre-purchased Mule Credit pool (Total credits), what's been consumed, and the Order End Date — no rollover past that date. Set Total credits and Consumed from the Wallet's Consumption Card, then use this tool to price a month's usage and see what remains. Rates, tiers and entitlements can change — verify the current rate card and your Order Form with your account team. Saved months persist in this browser only.
